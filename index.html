What is CGPA and GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the average of the grades obtained by a student in a single semester or term. It is calculated by dividing the total grade points by total credit hours.

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of all GPAs earned by a student across all semesters or terms. It reflects the overall academic performance of the student.

How to Calculate GPA and CGPA

GPA Calculation: GPA = Total Grade Points / Total Credit Hours

CGPA Calculation: CGPA = Sum of (GPA × Credit Hours) for All Semesters / Total Credit Hours Across All Semesters

How CGPA and GPA Help Students

- Academic Progress Tracking: CGPA and GPA help students monitor their academic performance over time. - Scholarship Eligibility: Many scholarships require a minimum CGPA or GPA. - Graduation Requirements: Universities often set a minimum CGPA for graduation. - Career Opportunities: Employers and graduate schools often consider CGPA as a measure of a student's dedication and ability.
